Privacy policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026

AgentProbe scans and assessments are run by Naren Ranjith ("we"). This policy covers this website and the paid scan and assessment services. It does not cover the open-source AgentProbe tool when you run it yourself: that runs on your computer and sends us nothing.

Contact for privacy questions and requests: narframes1@gmail.com

What we collect, and why

WhatWhenWhy we need it
Your name, email address and companyWhen you orderTo deliver the report and answer your questions
Payment detailsAt checkoutStripe, our payment processor, handles the payment. We never see your full card number.
Your agent's endpoint, request format and a scan tokenOn the intake formTo send the test requests you authorized
Your agent's replies to the test requestsDuring the scanTo decide what each attack achieved and to write your report
Emails you send usWhen you write to usTo reply and to keep a record of your authorization

Please use a staging endpoint with no real customer data. If your agent returns personal data in its replies, that data becomes part of what we process.

This website

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising trackers. It is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads its fonts from Google Fonts. Like any web host, both receive your IP address when your browser loads the page.

Who else sees your data

  • The AI model provider that scores replies. Each of your agent's replies is sent, with the attack that produced it, to an AI model that gives a first verdict. The model is run by an outside provider (currently Groq).
  • Stripe, for the payment. Stripe handles your card details under its own privacy policy.
  • Our email provider, for messages between us.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use your agent's replies to train models. We disclose data to authorities only when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Your agent's replies and your scan token: deleted within 30 days of delivering the report. We confirm the deletion in writing.
  • The report: sent to you. We keep a copy for 12 months in case you ask about it or order a retest.
  • Order and payment records: kept as long as tax law requires.

Your choices

You can ask us for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to the contact address above and we will answer within 30 days. If you are in the EU, the UK or California, the law gives you these rights and others, including the right to complain to your data protection authority. Lawyer to review: legal bases and regional rights

Security

Scan tokens and replies are stored on the computer that runs the scan and are not put in any public repository. We ask for a token you can revoke, and we ask you to revoke it when the scan is done. No method of storage is perfectly secure.

Children

This service is for businesses. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the effective date and tell current customers by email.
