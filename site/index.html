How an assessment works

  1. ScopeA 30-minute call and a signed statement of work that authorizes the test.
  2. Scan49 attacks through your agent's API, plus a policy review of up to two tools.
  3. ReportEvery finding read by hand, false positives set aside, missed leaks added, and fixes ranked by layer.
  4. RetestThe same attacks after your fixes, with the score change and what is fixed, new, or still open.

Attack packs are signed. Every report shows which pack, version, and signer produced its findings.
