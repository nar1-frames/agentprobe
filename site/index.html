What a scan can't tell you

Know the limits before you pay.

  • It is not a certification. It tests one configuration of one agent on the stated dates.
  • It is not a penetration test. There is no infrastructure, network or web-application testing.
  • The attacks are single-turn and public. Multi-turn attacks and retrieval poisoning are on the roadmap, not in the scan.
  • A pass can expire. A prompt change or a model update can undo it.

Questions

The tool is free. Why would I pay?

You can run every attack yourself, and many developers do. You pay for the human read and the report: someone checks each verdict, removes the false positives, adds the leaks the judge missed, and gives you a document you can forward.

Is it safe to point this at my agent?

Use a staging endpoint. The attacks go through your agent's real API, so anything your agent can do there, an attack may trigger. A run is about 50 requests, and we ask for a token you can revoke afterwards.

What do you need from me?

The endpoint and its request and response format, a revocable token, and written confirmation that the system is yours to test.

What happens to my agent's replies?

They are used to write your report and then deleted, with written confirmation. To score them, they are sent to an AI model run by an outside provider. The privacy policy has the details.
